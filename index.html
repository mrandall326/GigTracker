GigTracker Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 7, 2026

GigTracker ("the app," "we," "us") helps gig and delivery drivers track mileage, earnings, expenses, and estimated taxes. We built GigTracker to be private by design: your data lives on your own device. This policy explains what the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

1. Information the app collects

  • Location data. When you start a trip, GigTracker uses your device's precise location to measure how far you drive so it can calculate your mileage and potential tax deduction. Location is only collected while a trip is actively running (a persistent notification is shown the whole time), and it stops when you end the trip. Your location is processed on your device to compute distance and is not sent to us or any third party.
  • Information you enter. Earnings, tips, bonuses, expenses, expense categories, vehicles, platforms, trip notes, and settings you type into the app. This is stored locally on your device.
  • Purchase information. If you subscribe to GigTracker Pro, the purchase is processed by Google Play Billing and managed through our payments provider, RevenueCat. We receive confirmation of your subscription status and a non-identifying purchase identifier. We do not receive or store your full payment card details.

2. How the app uses your information

  • To calculate your business mileage and estimated mileage deduction.
  • To total your earnings, expenses, and estimated taxes.
  • To generate the reports you choose to export.
  • To unlock and maintain GigTracker Pro features for subscribers.

3. Where your data is stored

All of your trips, earnings, expenses, and settings are stored locally on your device in the app's database. GigTracker does not operate accounts or servers, and your records are not uploaded to the cloud or to us.

4. How your data is shared

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • Reports you export. When you tap Export, GigTracker creates a report file (for example, a CSV mileage log) and hands it to your device's share sheet. You choose where it goes (email, your accountant, tax software, cloud storage, etc.). That sharing is under your control.
  • Service providers. Subscription purchases are handled by Google Play and RevenueCat, who process purchase data under their own privacy policies.
  • Legal. We may disclose information if required by law. Because your records are stored only on your device, we generally have no data to disclose.

5. Location data, specifically

GigTracker requests location access "while using the app." Tracking runs as a foreground service so it can keep measuring distance with your screen off during an active trip, accompanied by an ongoing notification. GigTracker does not track your location when you are not running a trip, and it does not use background location for advertising or any purpose other than calculating your mileage.

6. Data retention and deletion

Your data remains on your device until you delete it. You can remove individual trips, earnings, and expenses inside the app, or delete everything at once by clearing the app's storage (Android: Settings → Apps → GigTracker → Storage → Clear data) or by uninstalling the app.

7. Security

Because your records stay on your device, they are protected by your device's own security (screen lock, encryption). Please keep your device secured and up to date.

8. Children's privacy

GigTracker is intended for working adults and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by an updated "Last updated" date on this page.
